# Shipping Policy

## Processing time

Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day are packed and handed to the carrier the same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends, ship the next business day.

## Domestic shipping

Standard domestic shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free for orders over $50. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $15.

## International shipping

International shipping is available to over 40 countries and usually takes 7 to 14 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the recipient and are not included in the order total.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed as soon as the parcel leaves our warehouse. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.
