# Warranty

## Coverage

All electronics sold by Brightline Goods carry a 12-month limited warranty from the date of delivery. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, normal wear and tear, or damage caused by unauthorized repairs.

## Making a warranty claim

To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order id, a description of the defect and a photo or short video. If the claim is approved we repair or replace the item at no cost, including shipping both ways.
