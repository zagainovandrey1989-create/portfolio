# Payments and Billing

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and gift cards. Payment is charged when the order ships, not when it is placed.

## Currency

Prices are shown and charged in US dollars. Your bank may apply its own currency conversion fee for cards issued outside the United States.

## Invoices

An invoice is attached to the shipping confirmation email. Business customers can request an invoice with a VAT number by contacting support.
